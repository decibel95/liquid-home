features:
  - title: Six cards
    details: Full cards for a light, a cover and a fan, a compact tile, a status badge and a room card that groups the lights, covers and fans of one room.
  - title: Drag to set
    details: One slider for brightness, position or speed. Lights get white and colour modes, covers open, stop and close, and fans snap to their speed steps.
  - title: Calm service calls
    details: Lights and fans send at most one call every 300 ms while you drag, plus a final one on release. A cover sends one call when you let go. The value you sent stays on screen until Home Assistant reports it back.
  - title: Six languages
    details: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Italian, following your Home Assistant profile.
