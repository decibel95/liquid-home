# Languages {#languages}

English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Italian. The card follows the language of your Home Assistant profile and falls back to English. The media card, and the media players in the tile, badge, room card and sheet, are translated into all six languages, including the state words (`Playing`, `Paused`, `Buffering`).

To fix or add a translation, see [CONTRIBUTING-translations.md](https://github.com/decibel95/liquid-home/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING-translations.md).
